I'm Yogesh. I build the full stack: mobile apps, backend APIs, cloud infrastructure and AI features. I've been doing it since I was in school.

Right now I'm building Cohostly, a cloud for small apps and tools. You drag and drop a folder, get a live link, and choose who can open and edit it.

I've started several products. Some got real traffic, like Corpintel, a free tool for Indian company filings that reached about 30,000 visitors. I shut one down myself when the market was too early.

I'm a problem-solution kind of person. Software is the tool I know best, but if a real-life problem needs something else, that's what I'll use.

I'm finishing a Btech in Computer Science at Bhilai Institute of Technology, Raipur, and I've worked with companies in India and Australia.
