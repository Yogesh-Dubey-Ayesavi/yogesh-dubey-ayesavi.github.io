Huddler answers questions while they are still being asked. That sets a hard budget.

Transcription takes part of it. Retrieval from the codebase takes more. Generation gets what is left.

Every step that could run early, runs early. Every step that could be skipped, is skipped.

Speed beat completeness every time we tested it with real meetings.
